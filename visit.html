What to expect

Coming for the first time

Bonita Orthodox Presbyterian Church is steadfastly committed to the worshipping of our Lord and Savior, Jesus Christ, and thereby bringing glory to God. We seek to bring glory to Him in the preaching of the Gospel and in living as faithful witnesses for Christ.

Our services are simple and unhurried. We sing, we pray, we read the Scriptures together, and we sit under the preaching of God’s Word. You will not be singled out, asked to stand, or put on the spot. Come, sit wherever you like, and worship with us.

What should I wear?

Come as you are. You will see a range on a Sunday morning — some in suits and dresses, others in slacks or jeans. Nobody will think twice about what you have on. confirm

Where do I park?

There is a parking lot directly in front of the building on Central Avenue, with accessible spaces close to the front doors. confirm

How long is the service?

The morning service usually runs about an hour and a quarter. The evening service is a little shorter. confirm

What about my children?

Children are welcome to stay with their families throughout the service — we are glad to hear them. confirm

Can I watch online first?

Yes. Every service is live-streamed and archived on our YouTube channel, and past sermons are collected in our sermon archive.

What is the Orthodox Presbyterian Church?

The OPC is a confessional Presbyterian denomination that holds to the Westminster Confession of Faith and the Larger and Shorter Catechisms. You can read more about what we believe, or visit opc.org.
