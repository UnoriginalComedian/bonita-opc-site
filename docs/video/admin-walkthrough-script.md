# Admin walkthrough — voiceover script

About 2½ minutes read at an easy pace. Each numbered scene is one shot in the
animation. **Leave a one-second pause between scenes** so the video can cut
cleanly. One recording with pauses is fine, or one file per scene.
Any format works (Voice Memos .m4a is fine).

Words in brackets are what the screen shows. Don't read them out.

---

**1. Intro**
[Homepage, top]
This is a quick guide to updating the church website. You can add events, post
news, and change a few other things right from the regular pages. No special
software needed.

**2. Signing in**
[Scroll to the bottom of the page; the "Admin sign-in" link is circled]
To start, scroll to the very bottom of any page and click "Admin sign-in."
You don't need to type any web address.

[Sign-in box appears; password is typed as dots; click "Sign in"]
Type the admin password and click "Sign in." You only have to do this once. The
website remembers you on that computer.

**3. The admin bar**
[Page reloads; the bar across the top is highlighted]
Once you're signed in, you'll see this bar across the top that says "Admin mode."
Only you can see it. Visitors see the normal website.

**4. Go to Events**
[Click "Events & News" in the menu]
Most updates happen on the Events and News page, so click "Events & News" in
the menu.

**5. Adding an event**
[Click "Add event"; the form appears]
To add an event, click "Add event."

[Date picker opens; a date is chosen; the title and location are typed]
Pick the date, type a title, and if you like, a location.

[Click "Add event"; the new event appears in the list; "Added." shows at the bottom]
Click "Add event," and it's on the website right away. Events sort themselves by
date, and once the date has passed, they move down to "Recently" on their own.

**6. Changing an event**
[Click the event; the form opens already filled in; change the title; click "Save"]
To change an event, just click on it. Make your change and click "Save."

**7. Removing an event**
[Hover over the event; the three-dot button appears; click it; the menu shows Edit and Delete]
To remove one, point at it and click the three dots on the right. On a phone,
the dots are always showing.

[Click "Delete"; the confirm box appears; click "Delete"; "Deleted. Undo" shows at the bottom]
Choose "Delete," and confirm. If you change your mind, click "Undo" in the next
few seconds and it comes right back.

**8. Posting news**
[Scroll to "From the bulletin"; click "Add news item"; fill in the three fields]
News works the same way. Scroll down to "From the bulletin" and click "Add news
item." Give it a short label, like the week it's for, then a headline and the
text.

[Click "Add news item"; the new card appears]
Click "Add news item" and it's posted. To edit or remove news, click on it or
use the three dots, just like events.

**9. Wednesday Bible study location**
[Scroll back up to "Our rhythm"; click "Change location" on the Wednesday box]
If Wednesday Bible study moves somewhere else, click "Change location" in the
Wednesday box.

[Type a new location; click "Save"; the footer location updates too]
Type the new place and click "Save." It updates on every page of the website at
once. Clear it out to go back to "the church."

**10. The sermon caption**
[Click "Sermons" in the menu; the "Edit caption" button is highlighted]
On the Sermons page, the video always shows the latest service from YouTube on
its own. You don't have to do anything for that.

[Click "Edit caption"; type a line; click "Save"; it appears beside the video]
If you'd like a line of text beside it, like who preached, click "Edit
caption," type it in, and save.

**11. Signing out**
[The "Sign out" link in the admin bar is highlighted]
When you're done, you can just close the page. You'll stay signed in for next
time. If you're on a shared or public computer, click "Sign out" in the bar at
the top.

**12. The full list (optional)**
[Click "Dashboard" in the admin bar; the /admin events list appears with search]
One more thing. If you ever need to search through a long list of past events,
click "Dashboard" in the bar, or type bonitaopc.org/admin into your browser.
Most of the time you won't need it.

**13. Outro**
[Back to the homepage]
That's it. Sign in once at the bottom of any page, then add or change things
right where you see them.
