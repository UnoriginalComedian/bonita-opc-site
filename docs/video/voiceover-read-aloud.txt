BONITA OPC WEBSITE: ADMIN GUIDE (voiceover)
Read at an easy pace. Pause about one second at each number.

1
This is a quick guide to updating the church website. You can add events, post news, and change a few other things right from the regular pages. No special software needed.

2
To start, scroll to the very bottom of any page and click "Admin sign-in." You don't need to type any web address. Type the admin password and click "Sign in." You only have to do this once. The website remembers you on that computer.

3
Once you're signed in, you'll see this bar across the top that says "Admin mode." Only you can see it. Visitors see the normal website.

4
Most updates happen on the Events and News page, so click "Events & News" in the menu.

5
To add an event, click "Add event." Pick the date, type a title, and if you like, a location. Click "Add event," and it's on the website right away. Events sort themselves by date, and once the date has passed, they move down to "Recently" on their own.

6
To change an event, just click on it. Make your change and click "Save."

7
To remove one, point at it and click the three dots on the right. On a phone, the dots are always showing. Choose "Delete," and confirm. If you change your mind, click "Undo" in the next few seconds and it comes right back.

8
News works the same way. Scroll down to "From the bulletin" and click "Add news item." Give it a short label, like the week it's for, then a headline and the text. Click "Add news item" and it's posted. To edit or remove news, click on it or use the three dots, just like events.

9
If Wednesday Bible study moves somewhere else, click "Change location" in the Wednesday box. Type the new place and click "Save." It updates on every page of the website at once. Clear it out to go back to "the church."

10
On the Sermons page, the video always shows the latest service from YouTube on its own. You don't have to do anything for that. If you'd like a line of text beside it, like who preached, click "Edit caption," type it in, and save.

11
When you're done, you can just close the page. You'll stay signed in for next time. If you're on a shared or public computer, click "Sign out" in the bar at the top.

12
One more thing. If you ever need to search through a long list of past events, click "Dashboard" in the bar, or type bonitaopc.org/admin into your browser. Most of the time you won't need it.

13
That's it. Sign in once at the bottom of any page, then add or change things right where you see them.
